Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computing that develops systems able to perform tasks such as understanding language, recognising patterns and generating content.

Key areas

  • Machine learning allows systems to learn patterns from data.
  • Large language models generate text from prompts and context.
  • Good prompts clearly describe the task and relevant context.
  • AI outputs can contain mistakes, so important information should be verified.
  • Ollama can run supported language models locally on a computer.
